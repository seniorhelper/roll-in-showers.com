Planning notes for the 60 × 36

A full package at the 60 × 36 footprint, the same nominal size as the ADA alternate roll-in reference, with marble-look "Venetian Marble" wall panels. It is aimed at households that want an accessible shower that looks like a finished bathroom rather than a clinical one.

Sixty inches long means it can go into many tub alcoves, but 36 inches is deeper than most tubs, so the bathroom floor in front of the alcove has to give up a few inches. Measure from the back wall to the nearest fixture.

Multi-piece walls carry through a standard door. Ask for the packing list to see whether trim, drain and accessories are included.

Before ordering

Five things to confirm with the seller

  • Exact model, nominal and clear inside size, drain position and entry height.
  • Whether walls, seat, grab bars, valve, hand shower or curtain are included.
  • Rough opening and floor support the installation manual requires.
  • Freight delivery, inspection on arrival and return terms.
  • Current installation instructions, given to the installer before demolition.
