01

Planning notes for the 51 × 39

An in-between size: 51 inches long and 39 deep. The 39-inch depth is three inches more than the 36-inch width of the ADA alternate roll-in, which gives a caregiver useful room beside the user, while the length is nine inches short of a full 60.

Sizes like this help in rooms where a 60-inch pan will not fit but a deeper shower is wanted, such as a bathroom with a window or door near the end of the old tub.

The low threshold is the right idea for wheels and walkers; ask the seller for the actual entry height.

Before ordering

Five things to confirm with the seller

  • Exact model, nominal and clear inside size, drain position and entry height.
  • Whether walls, seat, grab bars, valve, hand shower or curtain are included.
  • Rough opening and floor support the installation manual requires.
  • Freight delivery, inspection on arrival and return terms.
  • Current installation instructions, given to the installer before demolition.
