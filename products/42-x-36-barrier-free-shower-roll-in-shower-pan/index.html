Planning notes for the 42 × 36

A rectangle 42 inches wide and 36 deep. The 36-inch depth matches the transfer reference, and the extra six inches of width leave room beside a seat, which makes this a comfortable seated shower for one person.

It is a common size for converting a small closet or replacing an older rectangular stall. It is far shorter than a 60-inch tub alcove, so if it goes where a tub was, the leftover 18 inches becomes a wall, storage niche or bench area.

The pan-only listing lets you pick the wall finish. If you would rather have matching walls, the 42 × 36 full package in this catalog is the same footprint.

Before ordering

Five things to confirm with the seller

  • Exact model, nominal and clear inside size, drain position and entry height.
  • Whether walls, seat, grab bars, valve, hand shower or curtain are included.
  • Rough opening and floor support the installation manual requires.
  • Freight delivery, inspection on arrival and return terms.
  • Current installation instructions, given to the installer before demolition.
