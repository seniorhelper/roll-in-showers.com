01

Planning notes for the 60 × 36

A corner version of the 60 × 36 footprint with a center drain. The corner layout leaves one long side and one end open or finished, so the shower can be entered along the 60-inch face or at the end.

This is one of the larger pans in the catalog and reaches the 30 × 60 roll-in reference size on paper. It suits an accessible bathroom where the shower sits in a corner instead of a tub alcove.

Because two sides are open, plan the curtain track and spray direction carefully, and confirm the corner's hand (left or right) before ordering.

Before ordering

Five things to confirm with the seller

  • Exact model, nominal and clear inside size, drain position and entry height.
  • Whether walls, seat, grab bars, valve, hand shower or curtain are included.
  • Rough opening and floor support the installation manual requires.
  • Freight delivery, inspection on arrival and return terms.
  • Current installation instructions, given to the installer before demolition.
