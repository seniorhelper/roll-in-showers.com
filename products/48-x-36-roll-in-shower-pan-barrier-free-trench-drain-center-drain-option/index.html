01

Planning notes for the 48 × 36

Two inches deeper than the 48 × 34 and sold with a choice of trench drain or center drain. That choice is the main reason to pick it: you can order the drain layout that lines up with your plumbing or your floor, instead of moving the drain to suit the pan.

The 36-inch dimension matches the width of the ADA alternate roll-in, though at 48 inches it is shorter than that type's 60-inch length. A seated user fits inside on paper; a caregiver works from the open side.

If the floor will be tiled outside the shower, a trench drain at the entry edge can help catch water before it reaches the room.

Before ordering

Five things to confirm with the seller

  • Exact model, nominal and clear inside size, drain position and entry height.
  • Whether walls, seat, grab bars, valve, hand shower or curtain are included.
  • Rough opening and floor support the installation manual requires.
  • Freight delivery, inspection on arrival and return terms.
  • Current installation instructions, given to the installer before demolition.
