Hiring checklist

Questions to Ask a Roll-In Shower Contractor

The right questions show whether an installer has done curbless and accessible showers before. Ask the same questions of every bidder and compare the answers in writing.

Quick answer

The three questions that matter most

How will the entry be made flush or low, and what happens to the floor? How will the shower be waterproofed past the entry? Where will the blocking go for bars and the seat? Clear, specific answers to those three usually mean the rest of the job is in good hands.

01

About the company

  • Are you licensed for this work where I live, and insured? Can I see the certificates?
  • Have you installed curbless or roll-in showers before? How many in the last year?
  • Who will do the work: your employees or subcontractors?
  • Who is my contact during the job?
02

About the product and plan

  • Which exact pan and wall system are you proposing, and why?
  • Where will the drain be, and does it need to move?
  • What is the threshold height at the entry?
  • Will the floor be recessed, built up, or will a low-profile pan sit on it?
  • Where will the valve and hand shower go, and can the user reach them seated?
03

About water and structure

  • How will you waterproof the floor and walls, and how far past the entry?
  • Will you do a flood test, and can I see a photo?
  • What happens if you find rot or damaged joists?
  • Where will the blocking go, and will you photograph it before closing the walls?
04

About permits, schedule and payment

  • Is a permit needed, and who pulls it?
  • How many days will the bathroom be out of service?
  • What is the payment schedule? Avoid paying most of the price up front.
  • How are change orders approved and priced?
05

About the finish and warranty

  • What warranty covers the product, and what covers your labor? For how long?
  • What cleaners are safe for this pan and these walls?
  • Will you walk through the finished shower with the user?
  • What documents will I get at the end: manuals, warranty registrations, photos, invoice?
06

If funding is involved

If a VA, Medicaid or local program is paying, ask whether the contractor has worked with that program, what documentation they provide, and whether they will wait for written approval before starting. See funding options.

Common questions

Hiring questions

What should I ask before hiring a shower contractor?

Ask about license and insurance, curbless experience, the exact product and drain plan, waterproofing method, blocking, permits, schedule, payment and warranty.

How do I compare roll-in shower quotes?

Give every bidder the same written brief, then compare the product, floor and drain work, waterproofing, blocking, fixtures, permits, cleanup and warranty line by line.

Should I pay a large deposit?

Be cautious about paying most of the price before work starts. A payment schedule tied to completed stages is common.
