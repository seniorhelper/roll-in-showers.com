About Roll-In-Showers.com

Roll-In-Showers.com is a planning resource for wheelchair-accessible and curbless showers, published with Aging Safely Baths & More. Here is who is behind it, how the guides are written, and how the product links work.

Quick answer

The short version

Aging Safely Baths has installed walk-in tubs and roll-in showers since 2012. Installations are typically done in one day, and the company works in all 50 states, with supply-only shipping to Canada. It sells Hydro Dimensions, Ella's Bubbles, Bestbath (including RANE by Bestbath) and Meditub products. 01

How the guides are written

Accessibility numbers come from the 2010 ADA Standards and the U.S. Access Board. Benefit and coverage information comes from the agency that runs the program: VA, Medicare, Medicaid, USDA or IRS. Cost figures are national ranges from published consumer cost surveys, labeled as such, and are not quotes. Each guide lists its sources and the date they were last checked.

We do not publish invented reviews, testimonials, ratings, project counts or awards. If something on a page looks out of date or wrong, please call so it can be checked and corrected.

02

How product links work

Product pages on this site link to listings at Showers4Less, an online store for roll-in pans and shower packages. Those links are marked as sponsored. Product names, images and nominal sizes come from the store's catalog; price, stock, specifications and freight terms are shown on the live store listing and can change. Each product page here compares the listed nominal size with the ADA reference sizes so you can judge fit before you click through.

What this site will not do

  • Promise that a product makes a home "ADA compliant"
  • Promise Medicare, VA or Medicaid coverage
